# Payment policy

Official Authenticator releases are free to use. Official releases and upstream changes intended for them must exclude payment processing, checkout, billing, subscriptions, in-app purchases, paid upgrades, and features gated by payment. This applies to web, mobile, backend services, and distribution configuration, including dormant payment integrations and payment-provider credentials.

Contributors can open pull requests from forks. The payment integration check flags recognizable providers and billing patterns for maintainer review. Reviewers should also check for monetization that automated checks may miss. Changes to this policy require explicit approval from the repository owner before any paid functionality is introduced.
